# Hook Engineering Engine

This skill provides a repeatable cognitive framework for generating pattern-interrupt hooks that capture attention without resorting to hype or clickbait.

## Core Principle

Attention is not gained by information. Attention is gained by tension.

Hooks must create one of the following psychological triggers:
* Threat to competence
* Hidden failure exposure
* Future regret
* Status risk
* Pattern recognition disruption
* Contradiction of assumed belief

## The 6-Step Framework

When asked to generate a hook, you MUST follow these 6 steps sequentially:

### Step 1: Define the Core Message
Extract the actual point, the current assumption the audience holds, the blind spot, and the consequence of inaction.

### Step 2: Identify the Assumption to Disrupt
Ask: "What does the audience believe that I can destabilize?" Choose one specific assumption to target.

### Step 3: Select Hook Trigger Type
Choose exactly ONE of these categories:
1. **Competence Threat**: "If this keeps happening, it's not bad luck."
2. **Hidden Failure**: "The real problem isn't what you see."
3. **Delayed Consequence**: "By the time this becomes urgent..."
4. **Pattern Exposure**: "Most teams miss this."
5. **Reversal**: "Security doesn't fail during..."
6. **Regret Frame**: "You'll wish you saw this earlier."

### Step 4: Apply Structural Constraints
You MUST enforce these constraints:
* 8–16 words maximum
* No fluff adjectives or marketing language
* No filler words
* One sentence or two short punch sentences
* Must create tension but not make legal claims
* **AVOID**: "What if...", "Imagine...", "Let's talk about...", "Here's why..."

### Step 5: Compression Test
Verify:
* Can this be read in under 2 seconds?
* Does it challenge the reader?
* Does it force internal dialogue?
If no tension is present, rewrite it.

### Step 6: Output Format
Unless specified otherwise, generate:
1. 5 aggressive hook options
2. 3 executive-safe hook options
3. 1 platform-optimized version for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and YouTube Shorts

## Hook Scoring Matrix

Evaluate your generated hooks on a 1-5 scale for each dimension:
* Disruption strength
* Clarity
* Tension
* Authority tone
* Memorability

If the total score is under 18/25, rewrite the hook before presenting it to the user.
