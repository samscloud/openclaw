# Samscloud Trigger Events & Timing

This skill defines the specific events and signals that indicate a prospect is highly likely to be receptive to Samscloud's AI Security Infrastructure message. Outreach should always be tied to a trigger event whenever possible.

## High-Priority Trigger Events

### 1. New Leadership Hires
**Signal:** A new Superintendent, Chief of Police, or Director of Safety is hired.
**Why it matters:** New leaders want to make an impact and evaluate existing systems within their first 90 days.
**Outreach Angle:** "Congratulations on the new role. As you evaluate the district's security posture, how are you currently unifying your cameras and access controls?"

### 2. Bond Measures Passed
**Signal:** A school district passes a bond measure that includes funding for security upgrades or technology infrastructure.
**Why it matters:** They have budget specifically allocated for security improvements.
**Outreach Angle:** "Saw the recent bond measure passed for security upgrades. As you plan those investments, are you looking at replacing hardware or unifying your existing systems?"

### 3. Recent Security Incidents (Local or National)
**Signal:** A high-profile security incident occurs in their state or a neighboring district.
**Why it matters:** Heightened awareness of security gaps, particularly around response times and coordination.
**Outreach Angle:** "Given the recent events in [Location], many districts are re-evaluating their incident response times. How confident are you in your current system's ability to coordinate responders during an active threat?"

### 4. Grant Funding Announcements
**Signal:** State or federal grants for school safety are announced or awarded.
**Why it matters:** Similar to bond measures, this indicates available funding for security projects.
**Outreach Angle:** "Noticed the recent state grant for school safety. Are you planning to use those funds to upgrade your camera infrastructure or improve your incident response coordination?"

### 5. Competitor Contract Expirations
**Signal:** A district's contract with a legacy app-based security provider (e.g., Raptor, CrisisGo) is nearing expiration.
**Why it matters:** They are actively evaluating alternatives and may be frustrated with the limitations of their current system.
**Outreach Angle:** "As you evaluate your security stack for the upcoming year, are you looking to stick with app-based alerts or move towards a unified infrastructure model?"

## How to Use Trigger Events

1. **Research First:** Before drafting outreach, search for recent news, press releases, or LinkedIn updates related to the prospect's district.
2. **Tie the Hook:** Use the trigger event as the opening hook in your email or LinkedIn message.
3. **Ask the Question:** Follow the trigger event with a NEPQ-style question that highlights the gap Samscloud solves.
